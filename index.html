Research

Projects

Polymeric Sponges for Hemostatic Applications

MS Thesis · Prof. Aasheesh Srivastava · IISER Bhopal

I synthesized and characterized functional polymers, prepared hydrogels through enzyme mediated crosslinking, and freeze dried them into porous sponges. I studied how polymer charge and metal ions affected clotting and material behaviour. Rheology, FTIR, UV Vis, SEM, blood clotting, and hemocompatibility studies were part of the work, along with coordination of the biological evaluation carried out by the team.

Patent filing in progress

Self Healing Hydrogels and Hemostatic Biomaterials

Research project · IISER Bhopal

I worked on rheological characterization of alkylated chitosan hydrogels crosslinked enzymatically. This was my first project where I focused closely on how a polymer network responds to deformation and how that behaviour changes with formulation. The work has now been submitted to Materials Advances.

Manuscript submitted

Biomedical Adhesives and Hydrogels

Research Intern · Prof. Aasheesh Srivastava · IISER Bhopal

I learned polymer synthesis and functionalization while working on adhesive formulations and hydrogels. I used strain sweeps, frequency sweeps, and viscosity measurements to compare formulations and understand their mechanical response.

Soft Matter and Self Assembly

Research Intern · Dr. Ahanjit Bhattacharya · IISER Bhopal

I worked with sponge phase forming lipids and archaeal bipolar tetraether lipids and learned how molecular structure affects self assembly and soft matter organization. I also gained experience setting up protocols and working in a newly established research environment.

Organic and Bioinorganic Chemistry

Research Intern · Dr. Debasish Manna · IISER Bhopal

My first research experience involved functional models of metalloenzymes and metal homeostasis. I received training in organic synthesis, purification, and basic spectroscopic characterization, which gave me a useful foundation before moving into polymer and biomaterials research.
